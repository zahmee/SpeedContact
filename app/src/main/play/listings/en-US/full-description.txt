Speed Contact puts the people you talk to most on your home screen, so you can reach them with one tap without opening an app.

Four widgets to choose from:
• Photo grid: photos of the people you choose, with their names.
• Names list: names on lines under each other, without photos.
• Folder: one icon that opens a full screen of your contacts' photos.
• Names list folder: one icon that opens the list of names, with quick search.

One tap for what you want:
Choose what a quick tap does in each widget: direct call, WhatsApp chat, WhatsApp call, text message or contact card. The other options open with a double tap on the home screen, or a long press inside folders.

Make it yours:
• Choose people yourself, or show the contacts you starred on your phone.
• Order them by hand, alphabetically or by last call.
• Three photo sizes and three shapes, and eight backgrounds including transparent and your phone's colors.
• In the names list: larger or smaller text, bold names, the number under or beside the name, dividing lines and line spacing.
• For people without a photo, the full name can appear in its place.
• A folder icon from a ready-made set or from a picture in your gallery.
• An optional bar with two arrows and a Show all button when not everyone fits.

Your privacy stays yours:
The app has no internet permission, so your contacts stay on your phone and are sent nowhere. No accounts and no ads.

Permissions and why they are needed:
• Contacts: to show names, numbers and photos in the widgets and update them when they change.
• Making calls: to start a call as soon as you tap a person. The app does not read your call log.

Available in Arabic, English, Urdu, Hindi, Indonesian, Turkish, Spanish and Portuguese.
